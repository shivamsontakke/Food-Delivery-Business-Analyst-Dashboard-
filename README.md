# 🍕 Zomato-Style Food Delivery Business Analytics Dashboard

An end-to-end business intelligence dashboard built in Power BI to analyze food delivery performance, customer behavior, restaurant metrics and operational efficiency. 

---

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project simulates a real-world analytics workflow for a food delivery platform. It tracks core business drivers such as order volume, revenue generation, customer retention, average order value (AOV), and delivery fulfillment tracking.

---

## 📊 Key Business Metrics & KPIs
* **Total Revenue:** ₹2.9M+ across multiple operational cities.
* **Total Orders:** ~3K+ tracked orders with status breakdowns (Delivered, Cancelled, Delayed).
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** ~₹828.
* **Operational Performance:** Average delivery time tracking (~44.5 mins) with on-time vs. late delivery splits.
* **Customer Retention:** Detailed tracking of new vs. repeat customer distribution.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Skills Demonstrated
* **Tool:** Power BI, Power Query
* **Data Transformation & Modeling:** ETL workflow, star-schema relational data modeling (connecting Orders, Customers, Restaurants and Date tables).
* **Data Analysis Expressions (DAX):** Custom calculated columns and advanced measures for KPI calculations.
* **Visualization:** Multi-page interactive reporting, custom tooltips, slicers and drill-through features.

---

## 📑 Dashboard Pages & Structure

1. **Executive Overview:** High-level summary of Total Revenue, Total Orders, AOV, Avg Delivery Time and city-wise performance trends.
2. **Customer Analytics:** Deep-dive into customer segmentation, distinguishing between new and repeat users and top-performing customer profiles.
3. **Restaurant Performance Analysis:** Evaluation of top-performing restaurants, ratings distribution, cuisine-wise analysis and revenue per restaurant.
4. **Delivery Insights & Advanced Features:** Operational tracking of delivery status (On-Time vs. Late), cancellation analysis by city, and year-over-year trends.
